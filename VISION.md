# Vigie

Lookout for Quebec City. Not a priest of Truth. Not a party.

## Vision

A Quebec City resident opens Vigie and sees what approaches their life — city → province → linked world — as claims with sources, contradictions held open, and impacts they can check (price, law, housing, trade, health, death). Not left/right. Not a truth-priest. A lookout that leaves judgment with the reader.

## Mission

For Quebec City first:

1. Aggregate — never invent the news.
2. Use a finite, published source list.
3. Tag: geography, topic, claim vs counter-claim, falsifiable impact.
4. Publish the ranking method.
5. Show contradictions side by side.
6. Leave assent with the reader.
7. Expand nesting only when that method holds.
8. Name the rent (how the lookout stays alive).
9. Record absence: who spoke and who did not, edition after edition — sealed, verifiable, never a verdict (`REGISTRE.md`).
10. Serve the record before the page: the brief is one view; the register, the Markdown twin and the delta are the product.

## Kill list (we will not sell)

- Unbiased / Neutral / Truth
- Inform the world
- Fight disinformation
- Left vs right as the primary axis
- Full Canada or full planet on day one

## Niche (v0)

**Quebec City** — nest city → province (Quebec) → linked world (federal / Ontario / US / trade partners only when they hit Quebec City impacts).
